# Customer Communication Plan

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Need | Channel | Rhythm | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Outcomes, risks, value, decisions | Monthly review | Monthly | CSM |
| Customer administrator | Actions, evidence, technical coordination | Email/ticket/working session | Weekly during onboarding | CSM + Support |
| Customer security lead | Coverage, exceptions, risk | Review meeting | At pilot and completion | Customer admin + CSM |
| Canonical Support simulation | Reproducible evidence and impact | Ticket | As required | CSM coordinates |
| Sales simulation | Objectives, expansion signals, relationship risk | Internal account review | Monthly | CSM |
| Product simulation | Structured feedback and use-case context | Feedback log | Monthly | CSM |

## Communication principles

- Lead with customer impact, not internal activity.
- Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
- Use exact owners, dates, and next steps.
- Avoid promising resolution times outside an agreed service commitment.
- Translate technical findings into business meaning.
- Confirm closure with the customer.

## Sample onboarding follow-up

**Subject:** Northstar Apps Ubuntu Pro onboarding — pilot actions

Thank you for confirming the pilot scope. We agreed to begin with three non-critical machines so the team can validate the process before the wider rollout. The customer administrator owns the pilot change window, while the simulated CSM will update the adoption tracker and coordinate any support questions.

The next checkpoint is 18 November 2026. At that meeting we will review validation results, open issues, and readiness for Wave 1. Please do not send credentials or subscription tokens by email or place them in the project repository.

## Sample risk notification

**Subject:** Action required — seven machines remain outside the onboarding plan

Seven of the 25 machines are not yet scheduled for onboarding. This does not confirm a security incident, but it may delay the agreed coverage objective. The current blocker is missing ownership for four machines and an unconfirmed maintenance window for three.

Requested decision: confirm the machine owners and approve a maintenance window by 20 November 2026. If the date is missed, we will mark the 30-day onboarding milestone at risk and agree a revised plan.

**Subject:** Ticket NST-003 update — repository access restored for pilot machines

Repository access is now working on the three pilot machines after the customer corrected the approved proxy configuration. Support evidence shows successful name resolution and package index retrieval. The customer administrator will monitor the next scheduled update before confirming closure.

Next update: 15 November 2026 at 15:00 PKT, or earlier if the issue returns.
