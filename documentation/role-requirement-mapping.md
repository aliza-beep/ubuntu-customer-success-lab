# Role Requirement Mapping

This matrix connects the Graduate Customer Success Manager responsibilities in the supplied job description to evidence in this independent portfolio. It does not claim employment or real customer delivery.

| Role responsibility or quality | Portfolio evidence | What Aliza should be ready to explain |
|---|---|---|
| Find solutions to ticket requests | Five-ticket CSV, troubleshooting knowledge base, support bundle script | How impact, evidence, severity, and ownership guide the response |
| Improve problem-solving and onboarding documentation | Onboarding guide, knowledge base, README | How documentation reduces repeated effort and customer uncertainty |
| Drive digital customer campaigns | Three-touch Ubuntu Pro adoption campaign | Audience, objective, segmentation, call to action, and success measures |
| Identify high-potential and high-risk customers | Health model, risk radar, champion criteria in campaign | Why a numerical score needs qualitative context |
| Onboard customers and introduce products/support | 30-day onboarding journey and product learning plan | How to move from discovery to pilot, rollout, enablement, and value review |
| Understand Ubuntu and open-source infrastructure products | Ubuntu scripts and product overview | Business value and appropriate discovery questions—not unsupported technical claims |
| Collaborate with Sales and Support | Escalation ownership model and monthly report | The different responsibilities of CSM, Support, Sales, and customer administrators |
| Maintain risk visibility across a portfolio | Dashboard, report, health model | How to make risk actionable using owner, due date, evidence, and business impact |
| Collect feedback for Product | Structured feedback CSV and dashboard example | Difference between a complaint and useful product feedback |
| Customer-facing experience and empathy | Communication plan and sample updates | How to acknowledge impact without guessing or overpromising |
| Presentation skills | Five-minute demonstration guide and dashboard | How to explain the scenario, judgement, evidence, and limitations concisely |
| Organisation and documentation | Repository structure, consistent dates, owners, and status | How the project stays current and auditable |
| Teamwork across departments | RACI-style ownership table | When to coordinate, consult, inform, or escalate |
