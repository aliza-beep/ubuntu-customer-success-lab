# Five-Minute Portfolio Demonstration

## 0:00–0:45 — Context

“This is an independent, AI-assisted simulation for a fictional company adopting Ubuntu Pro across 25 machines. I designed it to practise the coordination work of a graduate Customer Success Manager: onboarding, support readiness, risk identification, documentation, and value reporting.”

## 0:45–1:30 — Customer objective

Explain that the goal is not merely to run commands. The fictional customer wants consistent security coverage, an understood support process, and visibility of adoption risk.

## 1:30–2:15 — Technical evidence

Run the health-check script and explain each section: operating system, uptime, memory, disk, network, services, updates, and Ubuntu Pro status. State clearly that the script is read-only and that WSL is not identical to a production server.

## 2:15–3:15 — Customer operations

Show the five tickets, escalation matrix, onboarding guide, and knowledge base. Explain how business impact determines severity and how the CSM coordinates without inventing a technical root cause.

## 3:15–4:15 — Health and risk

Open the dashboard. Explain why the score is 76, why that means Monitor, and why seven incomplete machines remain the main risk despite no critical outage.

## 4:15–5:00 — Value and learning

Show the monthly report and product-feedback log. Close with what you personally learned, which sections were AI-assisted, what you tested yourself, and what you would improve with access to a real lab environment.
