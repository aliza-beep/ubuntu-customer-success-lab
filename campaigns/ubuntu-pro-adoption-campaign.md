# Ubuntu Pro Adoption Campaign

## Audience

Newly onboarded customer administrators in the simulated technology segment.

## Objective

Move customers from subscription purchase to verified first value: a confirmed inventory, successful pilot, understood support path, and measurable adoption plan.

## Success measures

- 80% of recipients confirm an onboarding owner.
- 70% complete a pilot within 14 days.
- 90% know how to open a useful support request.
- At-risk accounts receive a named recovery action.

## Touch 1 — Welcome and discovery (Day 1)

**Subject:** Start your Ubuntu Pro onboarding with a clear first outcome

Thank the customer, explain the onboarding journey, and ask for the business objective, administrator, fleet size, Ubuntu versions, target date, and support-process questions. Call to action: schedule the kickoff or reply with the nominated owner.

## Touch 2 — Pilot enablement (Day 5)

**Subject:** Validate Ubuntu Pro on a small pilot before wider rollout

Recommend a representative non-critical pilot, share the onboarding checklist, remind customers not to email credentials or tokens, and explain what evidence to capture. Call to action: confirm pilot machines and validation date.

## Touch 3 — Value and risk review (Day 14)

**Subject:** Review adoption progress, open risks, and next value milestone

Summarise progress, unresolved tickets, upcoming actions, and customer feedback. Offer help to address blockers and identify relevant next capabilities only where they support a stated objective. Call to action: confirm the next rollout milestone or request assistance.

## Segmentation

- **Healthy:** pilot complete and owner engaged — reinforce value and share the next adoption milestone.
- **Monitor:** activity started but deadlines slipping — provide a short recovery checklist.
- **At risk:** no owner, repeated blockers, or no engagement — use personal outreach and agree an executive-visible action.
- **Potential champion:** strong adoption, constructive feedback, and willingness to share learning — invite deeper feedback or a future advocacy discussion without pressure.
